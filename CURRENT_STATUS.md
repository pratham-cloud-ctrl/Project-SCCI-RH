## Main idea retained

SCCI_RH proposes implanted spinal recording, computational interpretation,
learner-specific or function-specific stimulation, independently observed
delivery and response, and feedback. Expert-informed **retained motor learning**
and **selected physiological automation** remain central. Expert information is
aligned to the learner rather than replayed as voltage; OFF retention is distinct
from assisted performance. The pressure reference uses external pressure feedback
and positive pressor authority. AstroSam's Mind-Link inspires the progression
from future vision to inspectable engineering, with explicit credit.

## Final integration results

[Methods and source boundaries](docs/stage6/integration-verification.md) accompany
[the machine summary](results/stage6/integration-summary.json).

- 114,426 repeated software assertions across 28 finite scenario groups.
- 5,184 modeled motor events and 1,200 pressure events.
- 13 compiled-core boundary/fault cases, four separate ideal-supervisor cases and six companion feature-validity cases.
- Motor first ON error: 31.414 mm unassisted versus 16.668 mm assisted.
- Final OFF: 5.605 mm baseline/neutral, 2.897 mm favorable and 34.845 mm adverse under the selected learning laws.
- Pressure disturbed RMSE: 11.871 versus 20 mmHg unassisted; recovery peak 129.999 mmHg against a 120 mmHg model target, approximately 10 mmHg overshoot.

These are conditional host/model results. Motor features are causal Python
arrays; encoded pressure packets exercise the production C parser. The unchanged
production core executes admission and phase-state behavior. Peripheral timing,
current delivery, neural quality and biological efficacy remain unmeasured.

## Actual target and physical-identity boundaries

The preserved v2.0.0 BT40F/nRF5340 image has a 53,648-byte flash LOAD extent and 98,000-byte
RAM extent, with the manufacturer's 11 pF HFXO setting. It is compiled and linked,
not flashed or executed. Neural acquisition occurs in OBSERVE and is inhibited
while stimulation is armed. No radio image or simultaneous live spinal decoding
during target MOTOR is implemented.

The current additive UART correction has a 53,776-byte flash LOAD extent and the
same 98,000-byte RAM extent. Its 70 independent image/source checks and 1,172
host framing scenarios pass. It is compiled and linked, unflashed; the framing
fix does not establish target timing or physical peripheral performance.

The reference keeps its eight boards at 1:1 scale in a 600 x 310 x 240 mm chassis.
The separate future can/header is 60 x 58 x 14 mm before leads and depends on
miniaturized electronics and qualified implanted interfaces. The latest power
allocation remains conditional 3.154150 W / 4.879920 h. Earlier compact thermal,
monitoring-precision and timing failures remain preserved.

The final Stage 5 native evidence includes 14 reference clearance gates,
14,365 reference material pairs / 248 exact candidates with no unexplained
intersection, all future interfaces and a 260-object source-preservation check.
Its portable checkpoint has 12,996 checks. Native evidence is distinguished from
portable verification of exact saved sources and receipts.
