## The project this verdict serves

Project SCCI RH proposes an invasive spinal cord-computer interface with the complete
chain **implanted spinal contacts -> acquisition -> computational interpretation
-> learner-specific stimulation -> independently observed delivery/response ->
feedback**. Its original two applications remain expert-informed motor practice
with stimulation-OFF retained-learning assessment, and sustained feedback for a
selected physiological function. The present physiological model addresses
pressure support. AstroSam's [Mind-Link](https://github.com/sam-astro/Mind-link)
inspires the progression from an ambitious future concept to inspectable theory,
electronics, software and physical design.

The complete deliverable is digital. Simulations, native circuitry and CAD can
examine parts of the proposed system under explicit assumptions. They cannot
establish real spinal observability, selective recruitment, human plasticity,
chronic implant performance or healthy-consumer benefit. The original 28-page
paper remains a read-only evidence and hypothesis map, rather than an original
experiment. Expert recordings would inform a learner-specific policy; the project
does not assume that replaying an expert's recorded voltage reproduces their skill.

## Motor mechanism and what would be needed for consumer use

A future learner would first perform a defined task while the system measures
their own response and establishes a usable task representation. A competent
expert's recorded performance could supply a reference, after separate checks of
task competence and representation accuracy. The controller would compare a
causal learner forecast with that reference, select a learner-specific permitted
intervention, verify actual delivered charge and observe the resulting motion.
The policy must preserve unavailable references, artifacts, clock/support bounds,
response domains and nonbeneficial plasticity alternatives.

The consumer ambition is faster practice that leaves a measurable benefit after
stimulation ends. Temporary assisted performance is a different endpoint. A
retention or transfer comparison needs matched practice exposure and resources,
truly pulse-free assessment, adverse/zero/dependent learning alternatives, and
separate evidence that useful spinal information or the expert reference adds
value. The present work establishes neither a human learning multiplier nor
expert-skill transfer.

The old stateless baseline fails the independent changing-learner recordings.
The revised ten-state observer is a bounded response to that failure, not a
threshold change: ten state means, 55 unique covariance entries, 101 learned
common prior/drift entries, measured calibration (64 s fit and 32 s temporal
holdout), known instruction, and a declared 60 ms synthetic movement prior. It
summarizes the whole accepted trial prefix, rather than only 75 ms of local
history. Its development result is separated from fresh confirmation. Its
1,638,400-byte maximum transition-cache payload alone exceeds the proposed 512 KB
target RAM; this is a desktop mechanism investigation, not MCU evidence.

The fresh sensor-noise confirmation uses 16 new noise pairs on 2.5 ms and 1.25 ms
body grids. Every declared support is available; all 915,200 coordinate residuals
are retained and independently audited. Maximum paired-grid differences are
3.231031 micrometers in observed positions, 3.941148 micrometers in forecasts and
5.284652 micrometers in residuals, with no changed horizon-budget decisions.
Four named families reduce to two distinct no-dose laws. The confirmation has
no dose and tests neither the 25 mm complete-correction gate nor retained learning.

## Why passing a baseline does not permit a pulse

The baseline gate remains 15 mm, including the unchanged 3 mm observation-label
allowance; the complete correction gate remains 25 mm. A proposed response has
both gain and dose uncertainty. For gain `G`, dose `d` and their half-widths
`u_G`, `u_d`, retain the complete product interval:

`u_response = u_G*abs(d) + abs(G)*u_d + u_G*u_d`.

Add the appropriate independently observed response residual and any qualified
learning-interaction allocation. Reference uncertainty belongs in correction
against the expert reference; it must not be used to enlarge the prediction-of-
actual-position residual allowance. Conditional covariance is not a substitute
for an empirical maximum error allocation.

The characterized inherited actual dose has measured fraction 1.01318359375 and
uncertainty **0.4411205888605442**, approximately **44.1% of nominal phase charge**.
Every inherited actual isolated response domain rejects. The nominal future
interval `1.012 +/- 0.074` and prospective 4% monitor interval are assumptions,
not replacements for that actual observation. Improving prediction does not
qualify an electrical dose interval.

The inherited dynamic calibration and response validation used `learning=False`.
A learning-enabled nonzero action may also change the voluntary coefficients;
adding a learning-OFF response kernel to a no-dose forecast does not bound this
interaction. Either an independently declared observed interaction allowance
must cover every affected support, or the active branch remains unavailable.
This gap must not be bypassed using private plasticity equations at runtime.

If a later qualified protocol launches a profile, actual monitor records become
available only after completion (1.0005 s, first processed at 1.005 s on the
5 ms clock). They cannot rewrite earlier commitments. A monitor or residual
veto cannot undo a delivered pulse. Total-position forecast authority ends at
1.100 s; missing targets expire under the support-age rule. Pending response is
not silently erased when the forecast ends, and no release beyond the measured
600 ms response window has been qualified. These are future active-path
requirements, not newly exercised evidence from an all-withheld run.

## Physiological automation: a conditional second mechanism

The pressure application asks whether feedback can maintain a specified state
through a selectively accessible pressor pathway after spinal cord injury. In the implemented model,
external pressure observations supply state, measured calibration identifies a
positive response, and the controller admits finite whole reference-train frames
while enforcing freshness, saturation and fallback. Sustained pressure support
is a sequence of monitored interventions and observed responses, rather than a
claim that the cord can control every involuntary function.

The study compares OFF and feedback behavior under modeled disturbances,
altered gain, delay, sensor loss and invalid/stale observations. A separately
tagged privileged oracle is diagnostic only and excluded from benefit claims.
Stop, finite hold and ramp fallbacks expose the tradeoff between unsupported
delivery and loss of support. Reduced disturbance error can coexist with delayed
recovery overshoot. Positive pressor authority does not grant active pressure
lowering; changing the input sign cannot invent a depressor pathway.

Pressure numerics retain 408 runs in 136 noise-matched groups, including 24
frame-sensitive comparisons. The reduced transduction assumes an averaged
monitored pulse-dose effect; continuous native routing, analog guard behavior,
real tissue recruitment and target-specific physiology remain future evidence.
Dense stimulation removes qualified continuous neural recording, so this branch
explicitly uses external pressure feedback. It does not demonstrate a spinal
pressure decoder, healthy-consumer usefulness or universal body automation.

## Concrete handoff to electronics, firmware and CAD

The [handoff](mechanism-to-electronics-handoff.md) records the detailed contract.
Its major design responsibilities are:

| Responsibility | Required next evidence |
|---|---|
| Spinal acquisition | Native differential acquisition/filter/ADC, 16 streams at 4 kHz, noise/dynamic range, actual support/availability clocks, clipping/missing samples and analog recovery. |
| Two-source output and routing | Actual drivers, HV polarity/contact routing, source ownership, simultaneous service, whole-profile timing and independent shutdown. |
| Actual delivery | Independently observed cathodic/anodic phases, net charge and recovery tails; leakage/bias, gain/offset, integration-window and interrupted-dose uncertainty. |
| Runtime control | Tested support/action domains, finite permissions, cancellation/expiry, missing monitor/measurement rejection, explicit reset/washout and pending-response state. |
| Resources | Revised component power, heat, buses, memory and worst-case embedded latency; persistent observer resources must not be hidden. |
| Populated CAD | Actual component/board/package/lead identities, parametric assemblies, dimensions, nominal fit checks and STEP/STL exports from the resulting circuitry. |

The first native monitoring cell contains 70 components, 245 independently
checked pin assignments, three editable sheets and zero ERC violations. Its
33-run behavioral study retains 462 phase/gap/tail/net intervals and independent
KCL/matrix-exponential checks. These are a concrete partial subsystem. They do
not earn the proposed 4% dose target, implement a shutdown actuator, complete
current drivers/acquisition or qualify an implant. Bias-current bypass, endpoint
settling/protection and power are unresolved. The production OPA391DCKR pinout
is corrected; its nominal SC-70 footprint still needs a selected PCB process
and land-pattern review.

The 24 V / 3 mA / 200 microsecond driver boundary is a reviewed design proposal;
native implementation remains pending. The target SDK example links, but full
SCCI_RH target firmware is not implemented. Stage 2's allocation solids and ports
are not populated engineering CAD. A finished implant, finished KiCad system,
finished firmware or finished populated assembly must not be claimed here.

## Finite conservative-study exit decision

This conservative qualification study closes when its selected digital investigation has an audited
conclusion and a concrete detailed-design contract. This requires the frozen
method/confirmation, complete new replay evidence and its independent audit,
interpretation of both applications and the explicit limitations above. A
verified all-withheld result is an acceptable negative exit; neither a favorable
learning result nor resolution of every biological uncertainty is a condition
to invent. New model revisions become separately justified extensions.

**Current decision: conservative qualification study complete within its selected digital scope.** The
[exit review](exit-review.md) and exact source-bound decision preserve the
negative actual-assistance result. The conditional future concept and full
Project SCCI RH remain in progress.
Even after that finite exit, expert-informed retained learning and real
physiological automation remain conditional hypotheses. Subsequent positive
claims would require independent spinal-information evidence, selective and
reliable actuation, observed response/interaction coverage, matched delivered
practice and pulse-free retained benefit. A future failure can narrow those
claims without negating the project's inspectable engineering contribution.

The final integrated manuscript, complete electronics/firmware, populated CAD
and private repository release remain later project stages. Video and website
work remain parked. Contribution and assistance records are preserved in
[`provenance/CONTRIBUTIONS.md`](../../provenance/CONTRIBUTIONS.md).
