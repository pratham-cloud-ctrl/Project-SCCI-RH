# From the original paper's experiments to the next SCCI_RH mechanism

9 October 2026. This review reconnects the current engineering revision to the
28-page source paper. The original source remains read-only. Its SHA-256 and
page count are already recorded in [the Stage 1 review](../../provenance/stage1-review.json).
Pages 11, 17 and 25 were also rendered and inspected in this continuation.

The intended product is still an implanted spinal recording and stimulation
platform, digitally embodied through mechanisms, native electronics, firmware,
engineering CAD and an illustrated manuscript. Expert-informed retained learning
and specific physiological regulation remain its two central applications.
AstroSam/Mind-Link inspires the tangible engineering format. Current synthetic
model failures are revision evidence, not a refutation of all spinal interfaces.

## What the experimental precedents contribute

The [primary-source audit](../stage1/source-audit.csv) contains broader access and
cohort qualifications. The following selected primary reports were revisited
for their concrete implications for this revision; they are not pooled trials.

| Precedent in the source paper | Experimental contribution | Consequence for SCCI_RH | Boundary retained |
|---|---|---|---|
| El Hadwe et al., source p.7, ref.7 | Short-term circumferential recordings in freely moving rats; the movement-decoding subset contains five rats. | Preserve a spatial contact forward model and compare neural features with measured kinematics. Test ambiguous fields and actual acquisition apertures. | Movement correlation is not proof of a causal expert command. Published bidirectional filtering/RNN analysis is not a ready-made causal online observer. |
| McPherson et al., source p.12, ref.22 | EMG-linked intraspinal stimulation during injured-rat retraining improved reaching relative to open-loop and retraining comparators; gains persisted through a three-week stimulation-free follow-up. | Match practice resources; compare contingent, yoked and misaligned interventions; report assistance and OFF retention separately. | Muscle-triggered recovery is not spinal-recording expert transfer. A roughly doubled endpoint is not a doubled healthy learning speed. |
| Nishimura et al., source p.12, ref.21 | In two monkeys, cortical-spike-triggered spinal stimulation changed corticospinal output; timing influenced the change. | Preserve processing/support/onset delay, phase controls and unfavorable plasticity families. | This is hybrid cortical recording plus spinal stimulation. Its timing intervals cannot be copied as an SCCI_RH learning law. |
| Phillips et al., source p.17, ref.13 | Targeted lower-thoracic EES with measured pressure feedback regulates blood pressure after SCI; fourteen intervention participants span four studies with smaller experimental subsets. | Retain the external-pressure loop, target-specific placement, positive pressor authority, disturbance tests and saturation handling. | It does not demonstrate neural pressure decoding, independent arbitrary pressure lowering or healthy-user enhancement. |
| Formento et al., source pp.9/12, ref.24 | Human/preclinical/model work identifies interference with proprioceptive signaling during stimulation. | Evaluate sensory cost and acquisition loss alongside assistance; do not treat dense stimulation as free information or automatically beneficial practice. | A single constant sensory penalty is a structural hypothesis, not a fitted human dose-response law. |

## The conjunction that the digital project investigates

The source paper itself identifies the missing conjunction on pp.12 and 25:
expert spinal information must improve an individualized learner intervention
in a way that survives removal of assistance. It does not report an experiment
demonstrating this complete chain. Its p.24 agenda makes neural incremental
value, timing dependence and OFF retention separate falsifiable questions.

The next library therefore requires both **observed task competence** and
**independent decoded-reference qualification**. A generator named "expert"
does not suffice. The idealized inverse-body generator is a separately labeled
synthetic benchmark; its privileged time constant is never a controller input.
External teacher references receive the same resource accounting, so neural
recordings do not acquire an advantage from extra calibration exposure.

The next predictor must use observed past movement/delivery and declared future
instructions or commands. Future achieved movement and future measured charge
cannot enter it. Complete assisted recordings, pulse patterns and contexts are
withheld before any feedback coupling. Dense-stimulation neural packets remain
unavailable whenever their residual qualification fails; external fallback is
identified explicitly. A nominal finite-domain pass cannot authorize changed
anatomy, posture, gain or sensory consequences.

For physiological automation, pressure is the current demonstrated precedent
and digital reference function. The paper's bladder, cough, sensory and sexual
examples preserve the wider platform vision. Each requires its own state,
measurement, accessible input directions and endpoint. In particular, triggering
a response does not establish selective delay or prolongation of its preceding
state. Those broader capabilities remain in the
[capability map](../stage1/capability-map.csv); this revision does not silently
replace the platform with pressure alone.

## A finite Stage 3 exit

Stage 3 should resolve the declared digital mechanism and its design requirements,
not wait for proof of human efficacy or an arbitrarily desirable learning result.
Its integration criteria are:

1. Admit or reject teacher representations on competence and independent data;
   retain unsupported references as unavailable.
2. Validate intervention-aware predictions at the support/horizons actually
   requested, including correction uncertainty and failed contexts.
3. Resolve the selected source/aperture sensitivity and ambiguity witnesses;
   make any surviving assumptions explicit.
4. Evaluate a coupled policy only within its earned domain, then repeat equal
   practice and separate OFF retention/transfer with the declared plasticity
   alternatives and failure controls. Unsupported intervention must abstain.
5. Freeze precise monitor, timing, routing and power requirements for the full
   native electronics/firmware design. Circuit implementation is Stage 4 work.

The current monitor's rejection is preserved. A prospective precision allocation
can be an explicit future-architecture assumption, but must earn circuit evidence
in Stage 4 before being called an implemented delivery monitor. This permits
continued ambitious engineering without presenting a conditional model as a
functioning implant or treating current component limitations as fundamental
biological impossibility.

As of 12 October, the new library, aperture study, finite response/baseline
studies and [connected pilot](isolated-pilot.md) have their own evidence records.
The pilot rejects all practice interventions after independent learner-drift
validation; OFF assessments show no advantage. That negative outcome is retained
as evidence. A drift-tolerant observed forecaster and a qualified delivered-action
comparison remain open mechanism work. The concrete precision/timing requirements
already support a separate [native monitoring investigation](../stage4/entry-plan.md).
