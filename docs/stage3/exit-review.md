## Alignment and scope

The project remains a proposed implanted spinal recording, interpretation,
personalized stimulation and feedback platform. Its intended digital body is
native electronics and target software with populated parametric CAD, supported
by an illustrated paper and a private reproducible repository. AstroSam's
Mind-Link is credited for the vision-to-engineering format. Website/video work
is parked.

Both expert-informed practice with assistance-OFF retained learning and
function-specific physiological feedback remain central. Lower-limb coordination
and systolic-pressure support are reference mechanisms. Piano, sport and other
consumer scenarios remain conditional applications in the Stage 1 capability
map; they are not replaced by a desktop enclosure or claimed as demonstrated.

The [finite roadmap](../engineering-roadmap.md) defines Stage 3 as a selected
computational investigation with an audited conclusion and a concrete detailed-
design handoff. Completion does not require inventing favorable assistance,
human efficacy or a generic learning multiplier. It does require preserving
negative results and making unqualified causal links visible.

## What the negative connected result means

The new actual-branch replay withholds every nonzero action. Its valid
conclusion is unavailable active assistance under the present evidence. The
confirmed external no-dose forecast remains a supported component result.
Actual electrical uncertainty and the missing learning-enabled response
interaction would remain distinct blocks. Equal OFF outcomes after no
intervention do not disprove the conditional human-learning hypothesis.

The zero-launch replay verifies its causal clocks, forecast sealing, independent
position guards, one-shot withholding and hard-OFF assessment. It cannot newly
verify nonzero delivery, post-dose response monitoring/cancellation or repeated
intervention-modified states. Existing isolated component/fault tests keep their
own narrower identities. No reader should infer those unexercised paths from a
green replay check.

## Handoff and remaining work

Stage 4 must implement complete native acquisition/protection, current drivers,
routing and independent shutdown supervision, phase/tail monitoring, power,
telemetry, target backends and PCB. The 70-component monitoring schematic and
33-run behavioral study are partial evidence; their 4% charge target remains
unearned. The restricted driver note is a reviewed proposal. The linked Nordic
example establishes tool readiness only, not complete SCCI_RH firmware or deadlines.

Stage 5 must populate real boards/components/package/feedthroughs/lead interfaces
and check nominal fit. Stage 2's 80 solids remain allocation envelopes. Stage 6
joins all stages into the final illustrated manuscript, reproducible release and
learning/defence materials. The current scoped briefs do not substitute for that
final paper.

Future biological observability, selective recruitment, plasticity, chronic
packaging and healthy-consumer benefit remain evidence requirements. Additional
model families or repeated-session assistance can be separate justified studies;
they are not an indefinite requirement to manufacture a positive Stage 3 result.
