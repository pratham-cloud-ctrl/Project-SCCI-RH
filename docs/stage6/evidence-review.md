# Evidence review for the integrated Project SCCI RH manuscript

**Last primary-source verification: 16 October 2026.** This is a focused
narrative audit of 17 original research reports and the official Mind-Link
repository. It is not a systematic review, a meta-analysis, a reanalysis of
participant data, or an assertion that every relevant publication through this
date was located. The original hypothesis paper retains its separate stated
cutoff of **30 September 2026**.

The [reference data](references.json) contains author lists, journal metadata,
DOIs, primary URLs, verification depth, cohorts and limits. The
[claim register](evidence-claims.csv) distinguishes published evidence,
conditional premises, design inferences, project results, speculation and
inspiration credit. Earlier [Stage 1](../stage1/source-audit.csv) and
[Stage 3](../stage3/conditional-concept.md) reviews remain preserved.

## What the evidence supports

The proposed chain is coherent enough to design conditionally: acquire useful
spinal observations, infer a selected task or physiological state, choose an
individualized intervention, stimulate reachable circuitry, observe delivered
action and functional response, and update the policy. Published work supports
separate links in that chain. It does not establish the complete SCCI_RH system or
its healthy-consumer benefits.

SCCI_RH may assume undemonstrated but defensible links. Each assumption must name
the required information or biological authority, its consequences, and a case
in which it fails. A premise need not be experimentally established before a
digital concept is engineered. Conversely, an attractive CAD model, a target
firmware build or a favorable simulation cannot establish that premise in a
person.

## Primary research anchors

The numbers below count subjects for the relevant experiment. Channels,
sessions, trial repetitions, survey respondents and cadaver placement are not
additional implanted clinical participants. Papers in a research programme may
share participants; their counts are not pooled into a larger independent
cohort.

| ID | Primary report and relevant evidence | Boundary carried into SCCI_RH |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R01 | [El Hadwe et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-75128-z): short-term circumferential spinal recordings in rats; five-animal movement-decoding subset. | Pooled offline bidirectional reconstruction is not prospective human intent decoding or an expert-skill library. |
| R02 | [Fan et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202303377): penetrating mouse ventral-horn recording over nine weeks; separate two-mouse movement decoding. | Penetrating recording results do not validate a nonpenetrating human lead or indefinite stability. |
| R03 | [Woodington et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adl1230): acute rat circumferential recording/stimulation and spinal electronic bypass. | Test input was experimentally evoked; passive implants and human cadaver placement do not establish chronic voluntary human operation. |
| R04 | [Capogrosso et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1688-13.2013): rat modelling and experiments support afferent-mediated engagement of motor circuits. | This mechanism does not grant arbitrary direct excitation or inhibition of every desired pathway. |
| R05 | [Rowald et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-021-01663-5): personalized activity-specific stimulation supported trunk/leg functions in three people with paralysis. | Motor restoration under stimulation is distinct from healthy acquisition or expert transfer. |
| R06 | [de Freitas et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04435-1): seven-person cervical stimulation feasibility study after stroke. | ON assistance, within-study OFF changes and later follow-up are separate outcomes; finger responses were not universal. |
| R07 | [McPherson et al., 2015](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1505383112): EMG-triggered intraspinal stimulation plus training improved injured-rat reaching with retained benefit. | Its recovery endpoint is not a learning-speed multiplier; groups were impairment-matched rather than randomized. |
| R08 | [Nishimura et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2013.08.028): cortical-spike-triggered spinal stimulation modified corticospinal connections in two monkeys. | This is hybrid brain-spine access; timing-dependent connection changes are not expert-skill transfer. |
| R09 | [Thompson et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3968-12.2013): peripheral H-reflex conditioning modified a spinal pathway and locomotion in some SCI participants. | This comparator uses peripheral stimulation/EMG, without an implanted spinal interface. |
| R10 | [Formento et al., 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-018-0262-6): spinal stimulation can interfere with proprioception in studied SCI settings. | Assistance can have a sensory cost; the effect is waveform-, task- and person-dependent. |
| R11 | [Lorach et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06094-5): one participant used implanted cortical recording to control spinal stimulation. | The celebrated mobility demonstration is explicitly hybrid, not evidence of spinal-only intent acquisition. |
| R12 | [Squair et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-03180-w): targeted spinal stimulation and pressure feedback formed a neuroprosthetic baroreflex. | Human sympathetic nerve measurements were peripheral; pressure sensing was not a demonstrated spinal BP decoder. |
| R13 | [Phillips et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-025-03614-w): 14 implanted SCI participants across staged trials informed a targeted pressure platform. | Anatomical targeting and external pressure feedback matter; command latency and physiological observation rate differ. |
| R14 | [Mekhail et al., EVOKE, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(19)30414-4): ECAP-controlled recruitment was compared with fixed-output stimulation in 134 randomized pain patients. | Recruitment feedback is not direct measurement of pain, motor intent or blood pressure. |
| R15 | [Hubscher et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm15166287): three implanted participants received individualized bladder storage/voiding programmes and training. | Home modes were user-switched; the study does not demonstrate universal autonomous bladder control. |
| R16 | [Mussa-Ivaldi, Giszter and Bizzi, 1994](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.91.16.7534): paired stimulation approximately summed tested frog endpoint force fields. | A task-specific primitive representation is motivated, not a universal human control library. |
| R17 | [Kathe et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05385-7): human rehabilitation observations and mouse circuit experiments support a specific spinal contribution to recovery. | Mouse cell-population causality does not identify a human expert-skill store. |

The motor branch therefore has empirical precedents for useful spinal
measurements, individualized movement assistance and activity-dependent
adaptation. **Its central untested conjunction remains whether expert-derived
spinal information improves a learner-specific intervention enough to increase
retained acquisition beyond matched alternatives.** A purely external reference
or a learner's own best trials might perform equally well or better. That
possibility is an informative outcome, not a reason to postpone the concept.

The physiological branch has a particularly concrete precedent in
**function-specific pressure support**. Its principal observation is calibrated
external blood pressure. Spinal recording is a separate proposed source of
context or recruitment information. Calling the common platform bidirectional
does not require falsely relabelling the pressure measurement as neural.

## Mechanisms and assumptions used by SCCI_RH

For motor practice, the expert supplies a task-conditioned representation of
successful timing or coordination. Learner calibration maps that reference to
reachable, individualized stimulation effects. A causal learner estimate and
independent movement/force observations support limited assistance during active
attempts. A declared adaptation model describes possible practice-dependent
changes. Delayed stimulation-OFF tests distinguish retained learning from
performance assistance.

Four premises remain explicit: useful causally available spinal features;
cross-person task-space alignment; useful selective recruitment; and a
favourable adaptation response. Added value from the expert's invasive recording
is a fifth, separate premise. These are allowed design assumptions, not fitted
properties imported from the studies. The [Stage 3 motor review](../stage3/conditional-concept/motor-evidence.md)
specifies controls, adverse regimes and implementation consequences.

For pressure support, an external sensor reports pressure, quality and observation
time. A controller compares it with a declared target and requests bounded
stimulation. A target-specific response map supplies the assumed gain, delay,
saturation and withdrawal tail. Electrical monitoring checks delivered action;
physiological feedback checks the body's response. A positive pressor actuator
can compensate only a reachable deficit. Reducing added drive is not an
independent ability to lower arbitrary elevated pressure.

The [Stage 3 physiology review](../stage3/conditional-concept/physiology-evidence.md)
sets out these assumptions and the distinction between external BP feedback,
optional ECAP-like recruitment feedback, and a still-unestablished spinal
pressure-state decoder. The bladder report is a useful expansion example,
without making it an implemented SCCI_RH control mode. Other bodily functions need
their own accessible pathways, measured state and finite operating conditions.

For both branches, the sign of a task correction does not establish neural
inhibition. A biphasic current polarity is not a biological command axis.
Recording availability during and after stimulation, artifact recovery,
observation delay, biological selectivity and chronic signal stability remain
explicit dependencies.

## Relationship to the original 28-page hypothesis paper

The read-only starting PDF has SHA-256
`a1784965d63060e3b0c11fd3c01c9f0b4fe0b007cc65c605c76164ee1d4bf103`.
It is a narrative hypothesis paper, without original human or animal experiments.
Its constructed learning and control curves are illustrative. Its reference
cutoff and this audit's verification date describe different things.

No significant conceptual mismatch was found between its relevant argument and
the current project introduction:

- **Pages 11-13** already require learner-specific mapping, distinguish retained
  acquisition from ON assistance, and state the missing empirical conjunction.
  The rat study's roughly doubled recovery is explicitly separated from a
  twofold healthy learning-speed claim.
- **Pages 14-15** separate value of invasive expert information from the value of
  a good external template or individually optimized controller.
- **Pages 16-18 and 24-26** motivate selected feedback-regulated physiological
  functions and conditional feasibility, without granting universal control.
- The paper's biological premises authorize conditional theory and digital
  engineering. Requiring the entire human conjunction to be demonstrated before
  designing electronics and CAD would add a requirement that the project did not
  impose.

The integrated manuscript must add one important engineering distinction that
the hypothesis paper did not yet have: the actual eight-board digital reference
and its full-size mechanical chassis are achieved artifacts; the compact
implanted can contains proposed allocations. A future miniaturized circuit,
thermal closure, qualified lead/tissue interface and biological efficacy remain
future dependencies. The new paper should not imply that packaging an allocated
volume proves those problems solved.

## Source quality and access limits

R01 Figure 5 describes a 21.29% baseline as uniform random classification across
eight classes. Uniform random chance would be 12.5%; 21.29% instead matches the
listed largest class fraction, 215/1010. This is a source wording/analysis item
needing clarification. SCCI_RH does not adopt that baseline, extrapolate the
headline decoding result to human prospective control, or claim a reanalysis of
the dataset. The useful precedent here is spinal information acquisition.

R03 needs careful anatomical language: its rat procedure is described as
epidural, while the human cadaver method involves opening the dura and passing a
dummy device intradurally. These are different preparations. Six passive rat
implants are not six chronic active recording systems. R07's continued daily
retraining during the stimulation-free period also matters when describing
retention.

Access depth is recorded per reference. Author full text/XML or author-hosted
manuscripts were retrieved for R01-R03, R05-R11, R13, R15 and R17. R04, R14 and
R16 rely on primary author abstracts; unverified detailed cohorts or parameter
tables are not invented. R12 uses the publisher abstract and accessible extended
data; its subscription main text was not treated as fully reviewed. Metadata was
checked through the DOI-linked Europe PMC record, with publication year,
journal/volume/pages and DOI retained. An indexed first-publication date is
labelled as such rather than used to invent day-level precision.

This focused audit does not reverify every broader indication in the original
paper. Sexual function, cough, bowel outcomes, sensory restoration and additional
clinical motor indications remain in the preserved source map; none is silently
promoted into an implemented or validated SCCI_RH capability.

**R18:** AstroSam's [Mind-Link](https://github.com/sam-astro/Mind-link) supplies the
engineering inspiration: express an ambitious neural-interface concept through
PCB design, firmware, a visualizer, fabrication outputs and a physical body.
Its official repository was rechecked at commit
`a391eb851ee624be675057380f4e3b701ebf6ee8`, dated 3 October 2025. The README calls
it a proof of concept and directs working EEG seekers to the separate
MindFlexFast-Mod project.

The [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5YxrpcqkTYA) is credited as the user's
direct inspiration. Fresh video/transcript retrieval was throttled in this
audit; this record does not pretend a new complete viewing. The introduction's
account of the ideal biological-interface assumption is contextual information,
not fresh experimental evidence. Repository files do not establish functional
implanted human interfacing.

SCCI_RH's contribution is its own conditional SCCI mechanisms, explicit information
and actuation assumptions, finite synthetic studies and counterexamples,
inspectable reference electronics/firmware, mechanical integration and traceable
engineering evidence. Published biological results remain the work of their
authors. AI-assisted synthesis and generation are distinct from the student's
project direction and any later personal understanding or independent work;
the contribution record should state actual activities rather than imply
unperformed laboratory experiments.
