## Read the engineering limits with the results

The current target records RHD frames only in OBSERVE and inhibits recording
while stimulation is armed. It does **not** implement the new replay's live
spinal-feature feedback during MOTOR. The host replay demonstrates conditional
software interfaces; it does not execute the target peripherals, boards or
biology together. The earlier uninterrupted 450k aggregate / 4k acquisition
schedule and 4% phase-charge target remain unearned.

The actual eight reference boards retain their real dimensions. They do not fit
inside the small future can. Its electronics, energy storage, chronic thermal
performance and qualified interfaces require a new miniaturized implementation.
The present radio/network-core image is unimplemented; the future antenna/window
are allocations. The 3.154 W / 4.880 h reference power/runtime figures are
conditional calculations.

No hardware was fabricated or physically executed, and no human or animal data
were collected for SCCI_RH. Retained human learning benefit, selective chronic
spinal access, universal physiological control and physical implant qualification
are not established. The pressure example uses an external sensor and a
target-specific positive pressor premise; it does not establish a spinal blood
pressure decoder or healthy-consumer benefit. CAD and native electrical rule
checks establish their stated digital properties.
