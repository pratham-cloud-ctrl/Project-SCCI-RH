## Original project artifacts

SCCI_RH's contribution is the particular synthesis and inspectable implementation:

- A full spinal acquisition-to-feedback argument covering **both** expert-informed
  retained motor learning and selected physiological regulation, with distinct
  sensor, actuation and biological assumptions.
- Dated evidence-to-assumption reasoning; fair external-information controls;
  conservative negative qualification studies retained alongside a separate
  conditional mechanism study.
- Causal computational models, predeclared comparisons, OFF assessments,
  neutral/adverse learning alternatives and fault cases. These expose the
  consequences of assumptions rather than proving those assumptions true.
- Native modular acquisition, stimulation, routing, independent supervision,
  power, monitoring/companion and carrier electronics; target-linked firmware;
  exact source/interface audit and populated board geometry.
- A true-scale modular reference chassis, mating/interface mapping and a separate
  conditional future implanted-interface body with editable geometry.
- Integrated illustrated documentation, reproducible artifact records and
  explicit contribution/claim boundaries.

These are original **project artifacts and engineering choices**. SCCI_RH does not
claim to have invented spinal cord interfaces, discovered a universal skill code
or established a working implanted consumer product.

## What was actually checked

Saved native files, connectivity, routed-board parity, finite computational
tests, target-linked binary identity, source preservation, exact file bindings,
nominal geometry, selected material intersections and declared clearances have
concrete evidence in the repository. Multiple AI workstreams reviewed selected
sources and implementation details; they share the project's tools and premises.
Calling a check "independent" means a separate implementation or source/receipt
comparison within this digital workflow, not an unaffiliated laboratory or human
expert endorsement.

The firmware has been compiled and inspected, but not flashed or physically
timed. The electronics have not been fabricated. CAD material assignments are
proposals; valid solids and nominal distances do not qualify sealing, dielectric
performance, anatomy, mechanical endurance or chronic implanted operation.
Clinical efficacy and faster retained human learning have not been established.
