01 / A shared platform

From spinal access to a feedback policy.

Recording, estimation, intervention and observation form one argument. Different applications require distinct anatomical targets; no universal body-control channel is assumed.

What is implemented?

Numerical plants, synthetic signals, control logic, a one-channel artificial-input/reference-output PCB, parametric enclosure solids and presentation scenes. Long-term recording, recruitment selectivity and biological learning remain unverified assumptions.

02 / Computational experiments

Two applications. Explicit assumptions.

A / MOTOR PRACTICE

Does assistance leave a learned benefit?

Calibrate a learner response, estimate a rhythmic phase, and compare the assisted task with later unassisted probes.

—

retained model RMSE reduction at assumed β = 0.8

The negative control β = 0 produces no retained advantage. This is a test of conditional model behaviour, not evidence of accelerated human learning.

B / PHYSIOLOGICAL FEEDBACK

Can feedback reject a pressure disturbance?

Observe external synthetic mean arterial pressure and apply a bounded pressor command to a reduced-order plant.

—

mean absolute error in the nominal closed-loop model

Low actuator gain, saturation and sensor faults expose failure boundaries. Parameters and target pressures are illustrative.

03 / Tangible digital design

A mechanism with an inspectable body.

Illustrative component envelopes. Native circuit connectivity is in KiCad.

Electronics and mechanical sources

A 5 V reference circuit conditions artificial signals and reverses a nominal 100 μA test-load current. It does not contain a complete implanted acquisition or stimulation system.

The 100 × 70 × 32 mm enclosure has matched PCB mounting coordinates, separate lid and base, STEP exports and automated geometry checks.
